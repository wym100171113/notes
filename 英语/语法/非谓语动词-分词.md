# 非谓语动词 — 分词（Participle）

分词分为**现在分词（doing）**和**过去分词（done）**，兼具形容词和副词功能，核心区别在于**语态**和**时间**。

---

## 一、现在分词 vs 过去分词的核心区别

| | 现在分词 (doing) | 过去分词 (done) |
|------|------|------|
| **语态** | **主动** | **被动** |
| **时间** | **进行** | **完成** |
| **关键词** | 正在做 / 主动做 | 被做 / 已做完 |

- the **rising** sun（正在升起的太阳 → 主动/进行）
- the **risen** sun（已经升起的太阳 → 主动/完成）
- the **boring** lecture（令人无聊的讲座 → 主动影响他人）
- the **bored** students（感到无聊的学生 → 被动受到影响）

> [!NOTE] 情感类分词对比  
> exciting ↔ excited, interesting ↔ interested, surprising ↔ surprised  
> -ing 表"令人…的"（描述事物），-ed 表"感到…的"（描述人的感受）

---

## 二、分词作定语

### 前置定语（单个分词放在名词前）

- an **interesting** book（一本有趣的书 → 现在分词：主动）
- a **broken** window（一扇破窗户 → 过去分词：被动/完成）
- the **falling** leaves（正在飘落的叶子 → 现在分词：进行）
- the **fallen** leaves（已经落下的叶子 → 过去分词：完成）
- a **developing** country（发展中国家 → 现在分词：正在进行）

### 后置定语（分词短语放在名词后）

- The man **standing there** is my uncle.（= who is standing there）
- The book **written by Lu Xun** is a classic.（= which was written by Lu Xun）
- The house **built last year** looks beautiful.（= which was built last year）
- I know the girl **wearing a red dress**.（= who is wearing a red dress）
- The problem **discussed yesterday** has been solved.（= which was discussed yesterday）

---

## 三、分词作状语

分词作状语可表示**时间、原因、条件、伴随、结果**，逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。

### 1. 时间状语
- **Walking** along the street, I met an old friend.（= While I was walking...）
- **Seen** from the top of the hill, the city looks beautiful.（= When it is seen...）

### 2. 原因状语
- **Being** ill, she stayed at home.（= Because she was ill...）
- **Exhausted** by the long journey, he fell asleep immediately.（= Because he was exhausted...）

### 3. 条件状语
- **Given** more time, I could do it better.（= If I were given more time...）
- **Working** hard enough, you will succeed.（= If you work hard enough...）

### 4. 伴随状语
- He sat there, **reading** a newspaper.
- She walked into the room, **followed** by her dog.

### 5. 结果状语（常与 only 连用，表意外结果）
- He arrived late, **missing** the first part of the speech.
- The hurricane hit the city, **causing** great damage.

> [!NOTE] 分词作状语的核心规则  
> **分词的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致**。  
> 错：~~**Walking** down the street, the buildings were beautiful.~~（buildings 不能 walk）  
> 对：**Walking** down the street, I saw beautiful buildings.

## 四、分词作补语

### 1. 感官动词 + 宾语 + doing / do / done

- see sb **doing**：看见某人正在做（进行）
- see sb **do**：看见某人做了（全过程）
- see sth **done**：看见某物被做

- I saw him **crossing** the street.（看见他正在过马路）
- I saw him **cross** the street.（看见他过了马路 → 全过程）
- I saw the window **broken**.（看见窗户被打破了）

### 2. have + 宾语 + done（使某事被做 / 遭遇某事）

- I had my hair **cut** yesterday.（让别人给我理发）
- She had her purse **stolen** on the bus.（遭遇：钱包被偷）
- We should have the car **repaired** as soon as possible.

## 五、独立主格结构（Absolute Construction）

独立主格结构的逻辑主语与主句主语**不同**，有自己的独立主语：

$$
\text{名词/代词 + 分词/形容词/副词/介词短语，主句}
$$

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 名词 + 现在分词 | **Weather permitting**, we will go hiking.（天气允许的话） |
| 名词 + 过去分词 | **The meeting being over**, everyone left. |
| 名词 + 过去分词 | He stood there, **his arms folded**.（他双臂交叉站着） |
| 名词 + 过去分词 | **All things considered**, it's a good plan.（考虑到所有因素） |
| 名词 + 介词短语 | He entered the room, **a book in his hand**. |

- **Time permitting**, I'll finish the report tonight.
- **His work finished**, he went home.
- She listened to music, **her eyes closed**.
- **The problem solved**, we went for a celebration dinner.
- **Spring coming**, the trees turn green.

> [!NOTE] with 复合结构  
> 独立主格常在口语中加 `with`：  
> - He stood there **with** his arms folded.  
> - She slept **with** the window open.

## 六、核心辨析

### 辨析 1：现在分词作定语 vs 动名词作定语

| | 现在分词 | 动名词 |
|------|------|------|
| **逻辑** | 被修饰词发出动作 | 表被修饰词的用途 |
| **替换法** | = that is doing | = for doing |
| **例** | a **sleeping** baby = a baby **that is sleeping** | a **sleeping** bag = a bag **for sleeping** |
| **例** | a **running** man = a man **that is running** | a **running** track = a track **for running** |
| **重音** | 后置 | 前置 |

### 辨析 2：分词作状语 vs 定语从句作状语

- **Being** a student, he couldn't afford a car.（分词作原因状语）
- **Because he was** a student, he couldn't afford a car.（状语从句：更正式）
- **Given** the evidence, the verdict was obvious.（分词独立作状语）

分词的简洁性使它在书面语中广泛使用，尤其在正式文体和文学作品中。

## 七、典型例句总汇

- The students **taking** the exam looked very nervous.（分词作定语）
- **Having finished** his homework, he turned on the TV.（分词作时间状语）
- I had my watch **repaired** at the shop.（have sth done）
- She listened, **her eyes fixed** on the speaker.（独立主格）
- **Not knowing** what to say, I kept silent.（分词作原因状语）
- The **damaged** goods were returned to the factory.（分词作定语）
- He rushed into the room, **shouting** for help.（分词作伴随状语）
- **Compared** with last year, our sales have doubled.（分词作条件状语）
- They watched the sun **rising** over the ocean.（分词作补语）
- **Everything taken into account**, the trip was worthwhile.（独立主格）
