# 听力训练7 听力文本词汇与表达精析

> [!abstract] 文档说明  
> 本笔记基于《听力训练7答案》中的对话及短文，系统提取并解析了其中的**核心词汇**、**高频短语**及**典型句式**。每个词条均标注词性、国际音标（IPA）、多重释义、派生词及原文或自建例句。采用 Obsidian 兼容的 Callout、表格与 Mermaid 流程图，帮助深度掌握听力材料中的语言难点。

---

## 📚 核心词汇详解

### 1. oversleep /ˌəʊvəˈsliːp/
- **词性**：动词（不及物/及物）  
- **释义**：睡过头，睡得过久  
- **原文语境**（对话2）：  
  > *I hope I won't **oversleep** tomorrow morning.*  
  （我希望明天早上不会**睡过头**。）  
- **派生词**：overslept (过去式/过去分词), oversleeping (现在分词)  
- **近义词**：sleep in, miss the alarm  
- **反义词**：wake up on time  
- **例句**：  
  - He **overslept** and missed his job interview.  
  - Don’t **oversleep** on the weekend; we have plans.

> [!tip] 记忆技巧  
> `over-` 前缀表示“过度”，如 overeat（吃得过多），overwork（工作过度）。

---

### 2. request /rɪˈkwest/
- **词性**：动词 / 名词  
- **释义**：  
  - v. 请求，要求（较为正式）  
  - n. 请求，要求  
- **原文语境**（对话2，女士建议）：  
  > *I'd **request** the wake-up call from the hotel reception.*  
  （我会向酒店前台**要求**叫醒服务。）  
- **派生词**：requester (n.), requested (adj.)  
- **常见搭配**：request sth. from sb., request that + 虚拟语气  
- **例句**：  
  - Guests can **request** a late checkout at no extra charge.  
  - The manager **requested** that we remain seated.

> [!grammar] 用法注意  
> `request that` 后的从句用虚拟语气 `(should) + 动词原形`。  
> 例：She requested that he (should) leave immediately.

---

### 3. gardener /ˈɡɑːdnə(r)/
- **词性**：名词  
- **释义**：园丁，园艺爱好者  
- **原文语境**（对话3）：  
  > *You must have a **gardener**.*（你一定雇了**园丁**。）  
- **派生词**：garden (n./v.), gardening (n.)  
- **例句**：  
  - The **gardener** comes every Wednesday to trim the hedges.  
  - He works as a **gardener** at the botanical garden.

> [!note] 文化点滴  
> 在英语国家，雇佣园丁是常见服务，但个人也可 DIY（do it yourself），原文女士回复她自己做所有园艺工作，说明她并非富有到雇得起园丁。

---

### 4. extremely /ɪkˈstriːmli/
- **词性**：副词  
- **释义**：极其，非常  
- **原文语境**（对话6）：  
  > *She's been **extremely** popular since she was a child.*  
  （她从小就一直**极其**受欢迎。）  
- **派生词**：extreme (adj./n.)  
- **近义词**：very, highly, exceptionally  
- **反义词**：slightly, moderately  
- **例句**：  
  - The test was **extremely** difficult.  
  - Thank you for being **extremely** patient.

> [!warning] 程度副词  
> `extremely` 比 `very` 语气更强，常用于强调情感或状态。

---

### 5. slip one's mind
- **词性**：动词短语  
- **释义**：忘记（某事）  
- **原文语境**（对话7）：  
  > *It completely **slipped my mind**.*（我完全**忘了**。）  
- **同义短语**：forget, be forgotten  
- **反义短语**：remember, bear in mind  
- **例句**：  
  - I meant to call you, but it **slipped my mind**.  
  - Don’t let the appointment **slip your mind** again.

> [!speech] 口语常用  
> 该短语为地道口语表达，比 “I forgot” 更委婉、更生动。

---

### 6. retire /rɪˈtaɪə(r)/
- **词性**：动词  
- **释义**：退休；退下；就寝（较少用）  
- **原文语境**（对话9）：  
  > *Mr. Liu is going to **retire** next week.*  
  （刘老师下周要**退休**了。）  
- **派生词**：retirement (n.), retired (adj.)  
- **常见搭配**：retire from (job), retire to (place)  
- **例句**：  
  - She **retired** from teaching after 40 years.  
  - He plans to **retire** to the countryside.

> [!tip] 派生词  
> `retiree` 指退休人员；`early retirement` 提前退休。

---

### 7. assign /əˈsaɪn/
- **词性**：动词  
- **释义**：分配，指派；指定  
- **原文语境**（对话10）：  
  > *She has been **assigned** to a New York office now.*  
  （她已经被**派到**纽约办公室了。）  
- **派生词**：assignment (n.), assignable (adj.)  
- **常见搭配**：assign sb. to sth., assign sb. a task  
- **例句**：  
  - The teacher **assigned** each student a different topic.  
  - He was **assigned** to the marketing department.

---

### 8. import /ɪmˈpɔːt/ (v.), /ˈɪmpɔːt/ (n.)
- **词性**：动词 / 名词  
- **释义**：  
  - v. 进口，引入  
  - n. 进口商品；重要性（较少用）  
- **原文语境**（短文11-13）：  
  > *60 percent of the thirty million tons of rice used in Africa is **imported** from Asia.*  
  （非洲使用的3000万吨大米中有60%从亚洲**进口**。）  
- **派生词**：importer (n.), importation (n.)  
- **反义词**：export /ɪkˈspɔːt/  
- **例句**：  
  - The country **imports** most of its oil.  
  - These **imports** are subject to high tariffs.

---

### 9. economic crisis /ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk ˈkraɪsɪs/
- **词性**：名词短语  
- **释义**：经济危机  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *Rice production was reduced in some countries after the **economic crisis** of the 1990s.*  
- **相关词汇**：financial crisis, recession, depression  
- **例句**：  
  - Many businesses failed during the **economic crisis**.

---

### 10. investment /ɪnˈvestmənt/
- **词性**：名词  
- **释义**：投资；投入  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *African countries stopped making **investments** in rice production.*  
- **派生词**：invest (v.), investor (n.)  
- **常见搭配**：make an investment in sth., return on investment  
- **例句**：  
  - Education is a wise **investment** for the future.

---

### 11. drought-resistant /draʊt rɪˈzɪstənt/
- **词性**：形容词  
- **释义**：抗旱的  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *These genes can be used to develop **drought-resistant** varieties.*  
  （这些基因可用于培育**抗旱**品种。）  
- **构词**：drought（干旱） + resistant（抵抗的）  
- **近义词**：drought-tolerant  
- **反义词**：drought-sensitive  
- **例句**：  
  - Farmers need **drought-resistant** crops to survive dry seasons.

> [!science] 学术背景  
> 文中提及研究者寻找与干旱相关的基因（genes involved during drought），以培育抗旱水稻。这是现代分子育种的重要方向。

---

### 12. discipline /ˈdɪsəplɪn/
- **词性**：名词 / 动词  
- **释义**：  
  - n. 纪律；训练；学科  
  - v. 管教，惩罚  
- **原文语境**（Supernanny 短文）：  
  > *The parents don't know how to **discipline** them.*  
  （父母不知道怎么**管教**孩子。）  
- **派生词**：disciplinary (adj.), disciplinarian (n.)  
- **例句**：  
  - Good **discipline** is essential in the classroom.  
  - He **disciplined** his son for talking back.

---

### 13. talk back /tɔːk bæk/
- **词性**：动词短语（常贬义）  
- **释义**：顶嘴，回嘴  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *The children **talk back** to their parents.*  
- **近义词**：answer back, sass  
- **例句**：  
  - Don’t **talk back** to your teacher.

---

### 14. put up with /pʊt ʌp wɪð/
- **词性**：动词短语  
- **释义**：容忍，忍受  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *She doesn't **put up with** any bad behaviour.*  
  （她**不容忍**任何不良行为。）  
- **近义词**：tolerate, stand, bear  
- **例句**：  
  - I can't **put up with** this noise any longer.

---

### 15. naughty corner /ˈnɔːti ˈkɔːnə/
- **词性**：名词短语  
- **释义**：顽皮角（惩罚孩子的一种方法，让孩子坐在角落反思）  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *She sends them to the "**naughty corner**".*  
- **文化背景**：在英国育儿节目《超级保姆》(Supernanny) 中常见，类似“反思角”或“淘气角”。  
- **例句**：  
  - After hitting his sister, he was sent to the **naughty corner**.

---

### 16. visual /ˈvɪʒuəl/
- **词性**：名词（常用复数 visuals）/ 形容词  
- **释义**：  
  - n. 视觉资料（如图片、图表、视频）  
  - adj. 视觉的  
- **原文语境**（对话17-20）：  
  > *Dr. White said he wanted us to use plenty of **visuals**.*  
  （怀特老师希望我们多用**视觉资料**。）  
- **派生词**：visually (adv.), visualise/visualize (v.)  
- **例句**：  
  - The presentation included helpful **visuals** like graphs.

---

### 17. assessment /əˈsesmənt/
- **词性**：名词  
- **释义**：评估，评定；考核  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *We might as well try them out when we're not being **assessed**.*（assessed 是其动词形式）  
  （反正我们可以在不被**评估**的时候试一下。）  
- **派生词**：assess (v.), assessor (n.)  
- **例句**：  
  - Continuous **assessment** replaces final exams in some courses.

---

### 18. resource room /rɪˈsɔːs ruːm/
- **词性**：名词短语  
- **释义**：资源室（通常指学校或图书馆中提供设备、资料、媒体的房间）  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *There is one in the **Resource Room**.*  
- **例句**：  
  - We can find the map in the **Resource Room**.

---

### 19. statistics /stəˈtɪstɪks/
- **词性**：名词（复数形式，常作单数使用指学科）  
- **释义**：统计数据；统计学  
- **原文语境**：  
  > *We also need **statistics**.*  
- **派生词**：statistical (adj.), statistician (n.)  
- **例句**：  
  - The **statistics** show a decline in crime.

## 🔗 重点短语与句式

| 短语 / 句式 | 含义 | 原文例句 | 同类表达 |
|------------|------|----------|----------|
| **If I were you, I'd...** | 如果我是你，我会……（虚拟语气建议） | *If I were you, I'd request the wake-up call.* | Were I you, I would...; I suggest you... |
| **It completely slipped my mind** | 我彻底忘了 | *It completely slipped my mind.* | I totally forgot; It escaped my memory. |
| **It takes sb. time to do sth.** | 做某事花费某人多少时间 | *It takes us at least one hour to get to the airport.* | It requires...; we need... |
| **be assigned to** | 被派往 | *She has been assigned to a New York office.* | be posted to; be transferred to |
| **stop making investments in** | 停止对……的投资 | *African countries stopped making investments in rice production.* | cut funding for; withdraw investment from |
| **be worn out by** | 被……弄得筋疲力尽 | *The parents are worn out by their children's bad behaviour.* | be exhausted by; be drained by |
| **sort out** | 解决（问题） | *...help them sort out the problems.* | work out; resolve; straighten out |
| **bring up** | 抚养（孩子） | *...bring up their children better.* | raise; rear |
| **put into practice** | 付诸实践 | *She spends time helping parents put her ideas into practice.* | implement; apply |
| **start off well** | 一开始顺利 | *Things usually start off well.* | begin well; get off to a good start |
| **in disorder** | 混乱 | *...sometimes are in disorder again.* | messy; chaotic; out of control |
| **go into detail** | 深入细节 | *Dr. White said we shouldn't go into that sort of detail.* | elaborate on; specify |
| **might as well** | 不妨，还不如 | *We might as well try them out.* | may as well; could just as well |
| **be in charge of** | 负责 | *You are in charge of it.* | be responsible for; take charge of |

> [!grammar] 虚拟语气注记  
> “If I were you, I'd...” 是典型的与现在事实相反的虚拟语气，**be 动词一律用 were**（无论主语人称）。

## 📖 典型长难句分析

### 句子 1（来自水稻短文）
> *With this research we try to see how much water exactly is needed for rice production and try to see which genes are actually involved during the event of droughts.*

- **结构**：主句 `we try to see ... and try to see ...`，两个并列的宾语从句（how much... 和 which genes...）。  
- **难点**：`during the event of droughts` 意思相当于 “when droughts occur”。  
- **翻译**：通过这项研究，我们试图查明水稻生产到底需要多少水，并试图找出在发生干旱时实际涉及哪些基因。

### 句子 2（来自 Supernanny 短文）
> *She doesn't believe in giving children a beating – instead she sends them to the "naughty corner" when they behave badly and gets them to think about their behavior.*

- **结构**：并列句，`doesn't believe in...` 与 `sends them to...` 由 `instead` 连接。`when` 引导时间状语从句。  
- **难点**：`believe in doing sth.` 意为“认为做某事有好处/正确”。`get sb. to do sth.` 意为“让某人做某事”。  
- **翻译**：她不赞成打孩子——相反，当孩子表现不好时，她会把他们送到“顽皮角”，并让他们反思自己的行为。
