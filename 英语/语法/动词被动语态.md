# 英语动词被动语态（Passive Voice）

被动语态的核心是**动作的承受者作主语**，结构统一为 `be + 过去分词`。

---

## 一、被动语态基本结构

$$
\text{主语（承受者） + be + 过去分词 + (by + 动作发出者)}
$$

- The book **was written** by Mark Twain.（书被马克·吐温所写）
- The window **was broken** by a stone.（窗户被石头打破）

> [!NOTE] by 的省略  
> 当动作发出者未知、不重要或上下文已明确时，常省略 `by...`。  
> 例：My bike **was stolen**.（不知道谁偷的）

---

## 二、各时态的被动语态表

| 时态 | 主动语态 | 被动语态 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 一般现在 | do / does | am/is/are **done** |
| 一般过去 | did | was/were **done** |
| 一般将来 | will do | will **be done** |
| 现在进行 | am/is/are doing | am/is/are **being done** |
| 过去进行 | was/were doing | was/were **being done** |
| 现在完成 | have/has done | have/has **been done** |
| 过去完成 | had done | had **been done** |
| 情态动词 | can/must do | can/must **be done** |

> 例句：
- English **is spoken** all over the world.
- The letter **was sent** yesterday.
- The project **will be finished** by Friday.
- The road **is being repaired** now.
- When I arrived, the room **was being cleaned**.
- All the tickets **have been sold**.
- The work **had been completed** before the deadline.
- The problem **must be solved** as soon as possible.
- The rules **should be followed** by everyone.
- Your application **can be submitted** online.

---

## 三、被动语态的特殊情况

### 1. 带双宾语的被动语态

主动句中有两个宾语（直接宾语 + 间接宾语），变为被动时通常以**间接宾语（人）**作主语。

- 主动：He **gave** me a book.
- 被动：I **was given** a book.（更常见）
- 被动：A book **was given** to me.（也可以）

结构：
$$
\text{sb + be + given/sent/told/showed/offered + sth}
$$

- She **was offered** a good job.
- We **were told** the good news.

### 2. 感官动词 / 使役动词的被动语态

感官动词（see, hear, watch, feel, notice）和使役动词（make, let, have）在主动句中接**不带 to** 的不定式，但在被动句中 **to 必须还原**。

- 主动：I **saw** him **enter** the building.（无 to）
- 被动：He **was seen to enter** the building.（to 必须补回来）
- 主动：They **made** me **wait** for an hour.
- 被动：I **was made to wait** for an hour.

> [!NOTE] 例外：let 的被动  
> let 通常不用被动，若用则变为 `be allowed to`：  
> He was allowed to go.（不是 He was let go.）

### 3. get + 过去分词

口语中常用 `get + 过去分词` 代替代 `be + 过去分词`，多表示**突发、意外或自身相关**的事件。

- He **got hurt** in the accident.（受伤）
- They **got married** last year.（结婚）
- I **got lost** on my way here.（迷路）
- She **got fired** from her job.（被解雇）
- The window **got broken** during the storm.（被打破）

---

## 四、不用被动语态的情况

以下动词/结构**不能**用于被动语态：

| 类别 | 示例 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| **不及物动词** | happen, die, arrive, go, come, sleep | 没有宾语，无法变被动 |
| **系动词** | be, become, look, sound, smell, taste, feel, seem | 表状态，不表动作 |
| **have 表"有"** | I have a car.（不能变 A car is had by me.） | 表"拥有"时 |
| **某些不及物短语** | take place, occur, belong to, consist of | 天然不带宾语 |

- The accident **happened** yesterday.（不能说 ~~was happened~~）
- This book **belongs to** me.（不能说 ~~is belonged to~~）
- The meeting **took place** last week.（不能说 ~~was taken place~~）
- The team **consists of** five members.（不能说 ~~is consisted of~~）

---

## 五、辨析：主动语态 vs 被动语态的选择原则

选用被动语态的三种典型情况：

| 原则 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 强调动作承受者 | The new policy **was announced** by the government. |
| 不知动作发出者 | My wallet **was stolen** on the bus. |
| 动作发出者不重要 | The products **are made** in China. |

> [!NOTE] 写作建议  
> 科技论文、新闻报道中被动语态非常常见，因为它显得客观。但日常写作不宜过度使用，否则显得僵硬。

## 六、典型例句总汇

- The book **was translated** into over 20 languages.
- Dinner **is being prepared** in the kitchen right now.
- Your car **will be repaired** by noon.
- The thief **was seen to run** out of the building.
- All the files **have been deleted** by mistake.
- The meeting **had been postponed** before I knew about it.
- The children **must be picked up** before 5:00 p.m.
- I **got stuck** in traffic for two hours.
- The Great Wall **can be seen** from space.（有争议但常见表达）
- A new hospital **is being built** in our neighborhood.
