# 英语数词（Numeral）

数词分为**基数词**（表示数量）和**序数词**（表示顺序）。

---

# 一、基数词（Cardinal Numbers）

## 1. 基本基数词表

| 数字 | 英文 | 数字 | 英文 | 数字 | 英文 |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | one | 11 | eleven | 30 | thirty |
| 2 | two | 12 | twelve | 40 | forty |
| 3 | three | 13 | thirteen | 50 | fifty |
| 4 | four | 14 | fourteen | 60 | sixty |
| 5 | five | 15 | fifteen | 70 | seventy |
| 6 | six | 16 | sixteen | 80 | eighty |
| 7 | seven | 17 | seventeen | 90 | ninety |
| 8 | eight | 18 | eighteen | 100 | one hundred |
| 9 | nine | 19 | nineteen | 1,000 | one thousand |
| 10 | ten | 20 | twenty | 1,000,000 | one million |

## 2. 构成规律

- 21—99：十位数 + 个位数（连字符）：**twenty-one**, **thirty-five**
- 101—999：百位数 + and + 十位个位：**one hundred and one**
- 千以上：thousand / million / billion 不加 s（具体数字时）

例句：
- I have **twenty-three** books.
- There are **one hundred and fifty** students in the hall.
- The city has a population of **two million**.

> [!NOTE] 注意
> hundred / thousand / million / billion 在具体数字后**不加 s**：
> - 对：three **hundred** people
> - 错：three hundreds people
>
> 但在短语 hundreds of / thousands of 中要加 s：**hundreds of** people（成百上千的人）

# 二、序数词（Ordinal Numbers）

| 基数 | 序数 | 缩写 | 基数 | 序数 | 缩写 |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| one | **first** | 1st | eleven | **eleventh** | 11th |
| two | **second** | 2nd | twelve | **twelfth** | 12th |
| three | **third** | 3rd | thirteen | **thirteenth** | 13th |
| four | fourth | 4th | twenty | **twentieth** | 20th |
| five | **fifth** | 5th | thirty | thirtieth | 30th |
| six | sixth | 6th | forty | fortieth | 40th |
| seven | seventh | 7th | fifty | fiftieth | 50th |
| eight | **eighth** | 8th | sixty | sixtieth | 60th |
| nine | **ninth** | 9th | hundred | hundredth | 100th |
| ten | tenth | 10th | thousand | thousandth | 1,000th |

## 构成规律

- 一般规则：基数词 + **th**：four → fourth, six → sixth
- 不规则变化：first, second, third, fifth, eighth, ninth, twelfth
- 以 y 结尾的基数词，变 y 为 i + eth：twenty → twentieth, thirty → thirtieth
- 多位数：只变最后一位：twenty-**first**, one hundred and **second**

例句：
- He won the **first** prize.
- This is my **third** visit to Beijing.
- She lives on the **twentieth** floor.
- He was the **twenty-first** to arrive.
- May is the **fifth** month of the year.

# 三、分数、小数、百分数

## 1. 分数（Fractions）

分子用基数词，分母用序数词。分子大于 1 时，分母加 s。

| 分数 | 读法 |
|------|------|
| 1/2 | **one half** (或 a half) |
| 1/3 | **one third** |
| 1/4 | **one quarter** (或 one fourth) |
| 2/3 | **two thirds** |
| 3/4 | **three quarters** (或 three fourths) |
| 1/10 | **one tenth** |

例句：
- **One third** of the students are from abroad.
- He finished **two thirds** of the work.

## 2. 小数（Decimals）

小数点读作 **point**，小数点后的数字逐个读出。

- 0.5 → **zero point five** / **point five**
- 3.14 → **three point one four**
- 10.05 → **ten point zero five**

## 3. 百分数（Percentages）

- 50% → **fifty percent**
- 100% → **one hundred percent**
- 25.5% → **twenty-five point five percent**

例句：
- **Fifty percent** of the population lives in cities.
- The discount is **twenty percent**.

# 四、倍数表达

| 表达方式 | 例句 |
|----------|------|
| twice / three times + as...as | This room is **twice as big as** that one. |
| three times + 比较级 + than | This box is **three times heavier than** that one. |
| double / triple | Our profit has **doubled** this year. |

例句：
- Her salary is **twice as high as** mine.
- The new bridge is **three times longer than** the old one.
- The population has **tripled** in the last decade.
- He earns **double** what I earn.
- This bag cost **three times as much as** that one.

# 五、日期、时间、电话号码、年龄的读法

| 类型 | 示例 | 读法 |
|------|------|------|
| 日期 | 2026年6月13日 | June (the) thirteenth, twenty twenty-six |
| 时间 | 8:30 | eight thirty / half past eight |
| 时间 | 7:15 | seven fifteen / a quarter past seven |
| 时间 | 9:45 | nine forty-five / a quarter to ten |
| 电话 | 138-5555-6789 | one three eight, five five five five, six seven eight nine |
| 年龄 | 25岁 | twenty-five years old |

例句：
- The meeting is on **June 13th, 2026**.
- I wake up at **six thirty** every day.
- She is **thirty-two years old**.

# 六、辨析

> [!NOTE] 辨析：hundred vs hundreds of
> - 具体数字 + **hundred**（不加 s，不加 of）：**three hundred** people
> - **hundreds of**（加 s 加 of，表示约数）：**hundreds of** people（成百上千的）
>
> 同样适用于 thousand / million / billion：
> - **five thousand** dollars vs **thousands of** dollars

> [!NOTE] 辨析：序数词 second 的位置
> - 一般情况下序数词在基数词前面：**the first two** chapters（前两章）
> - 固定搭配中顺序不同：the **last three** days
>
> 规则：序数词 + 基数词 + 名词（the first three questions）
