# 替代与省略（Substitution & Ellipsis）

替代（Substitution）是用替代词代替重复出现的部分，省略（Ellipsis）则直接省去可以不言自明的部分。两者都是英语中避免重复的重要手段。

---

# 一、替代（Substitution）

## 1. 名词替代

| 替代词 | 替代对象 | 用法 |
|--------|----------|------|
| one / ones | 可数名词（泛指） | 替代 a/an + 可数名词 |
| that / those | 可数或不可数名词（特指） | 替代 the + 名词（尤其带后置定语） |
| the same | 同样的事物 | 替代前面提到的同类事物 |

例：
- I lost my pen. I must buy **one**.（one = a pen，泛指一支新笔，不是原来那支。）
- These apples are too small. Give me some big **ones**.（ones = apples，泛指大的苹果。）
- The weather in Beijing is colder than **that** in Shanghai.（that = the weather，特指上海天气。）
- The cars made in Japan are better than **those** made in Korea.（those = the cars，特指韩国造的车。）
- —I want a cup of coffee. —Make mine **the same**.（the same = a cup of coffee，同样一杯咖啡。）

## 2. 动词替代

do / does / did 替代前面出现过的**动词或动词短语**。

- He runs faster than I **do**.（do 替代 run）
- She likes music more than her husband **does**.（does 替代 likes music）
- He came earlier than I **did**.（did 替代 came）
- —You broke the window! —No, I **didn't**!（didn't 替代 didn't break the window）
- She promised to help, and she **did**.（did 替代 helped）

## 3. 分句替代

| 替代词 | 替代对象 | 常见搭配 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| so | 肯定的 that 从句 | think / suppose / hope / believe / expect / be afraid + so |
| not | 否定的 that 从句 | think / suppose / hope / believe / expect / be afraid + not |

例：
- —Is he coming? —I think **so**.（so = that he is coming）
- —Will it rain tomorrow? —I hope **not**.（not = that it will not rain）
- —Are they going to win? —I'm afraid **not**.（not = that they are not going to win）
- —Do you think she will pass the exam? —I believe **so**.（so = that she will pass the exam）

> [!NOTE] 否定转移注意
> - I don't think **so**. = I think **not**.
> - I don't suppose **so**.（更常见；I suppose **not** 也可以）
> - hope 只有 I hope **not**，没有 I don't hope so.

# 二、省略（Ellipsis）

## 1. 并列句中的省略

并列句中，后一分句中与前一分句相同的**主语、谓语或宾语**可省略。

- He is a doctor and (he is) a teacher.（省略主语 + be 动词）
- She likes tea but (she) hates coffee.（省略主语）
- Tom will go to the party and (Tom will) take his sister.（省略主语 + 助动词）
- I like English and (I like) history.（省略主语 + 谓语）
- She cleaned the room and (she) cooked dinner.（省略主语）

## 2. 状语从句中的省略

当状语从句的主语与主句主语一致，且从句谓语中含有 be 动词时，可省略**主语 + be 动词**。

| 连词 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| when | When (he was) asked, he kept silent. |
| while | While (I was) reading, I fell asleep. |
| if | If (it is) possible, I'll come. |
| unless | Unless (you are) invited, don't go. |
| though/although | Though (he is) young, he knows a lot. |
| once | Once (it is) seen, it can never be forgotten. |

例：
- When **(he was) asked**, he kept silent.（当被问到时，他保持沉默。）
- If **(it is) possible**, I'll come.（如果可能，我会来。）
- While **(I was) walking** in the street, I met her.（在街上走的时候，我遇见了她。）
- Though **(he is) tired**, he went on working.（虽然累了，他还是继续工作。）

## 3. 不定式的省略

为避免重复，用 **to** 代替整个不定式短语。

- —Would you like to go with me? —I'd love **to** (go with you).（我很乐意去。）
- —Why didn't you come? —I forgot **to** (come).（我忘了来。）
- He wants to go abroad, but his parents don't want him **to** (go abroad).（他父母不想让他出国。）
- You can come if you want **to** (come).（你想来就来。）
- —Have you finished your homework? —I'm about **to** (finish it).（我正要完成。）

## 4. 比较状语从句中的省略

在比较状语从句中，与主句相同的部分常省略。

- He is taller than I (am tall).（他比我高。）
- She works as hard as he (does).（她和他一样努力。）
- The exam was more difficult than (we) expected.（考试比我们预期的更难。）
- He arrived earlier than (he was) usual.（他到得比平时早。）

# 三、辨析：one vs that vs it

| 替代词 | 指代对象 | 同类与否 | 用法 |
|--------|----------|:------:|------|
| one | 泛指同类中的**另一个** | 同类不同物 | 替代 a/an + 可数名词 |
| that | 特指同类中的**那个** | 同类不同物 | 替代 the + 名词（常带定语） |
| it | 指代**前面提到的同一事物** | 同一事物 | 就是指那个东西 |

例：
- I lost my pen. I must buy **one**.（买一支新的，不是原来那支。）
- The climate in Beijing is colder than **that** in Shanghai.（that = the climate，指上海的气候，不是北京的。）
- I have a pen. **It** is black.（it 就是前面提到的那支笔，同一支。）
- Your story is funny; I have never heard **one** like that.（一个像那样的故事，同类不同物。）
- The population of China is larger than **that** of Japan.（that = the population，特指日本的人口。）

> [!NOTE] 快速判断
> - 是不是**同一物体**？是 → it
> - 指代 **the + 名词**（带修饰语）？是 → that
> - 泛指 **a/an + 名词**？是 → one

# 四、典型例句总汇

**替代：**
- This shirt is too small. Show me a bigger **one**.
- The houses in the city are taller than **those** in the countryside.
- He speaks English better than I **do**.
- —Will she come tomorrow? —I hope **so**.
- —Is he going to fail? —I'm afraid **not**.

**省略：**
- She can sing and (she can) dance.（并列句省略）
- When (he was) asked, he gave no answer.（状语从句省略）
- —Do you want to join us? —I'd be glad **to**.（不定式省略）
- He is richer than I (am).（比较状语从句省略）
- If (it is) necessary, call me at once.（状语从句省略）
