# 英语副词（Adverb）

副词用于修饰动词、形容词、其他副词或整个句子，表示时间、地点、方式、程度等。

---

# 一、副词分类

## 1. 方式副词（Adverbs of Manner）

回答"怎样地？"

| 副词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| quickly | She ran **quickly** to catch the bus. |
| carefully | He drove **carefully** in the rain. |
| well | She speaks English **well**. |
| happily | They lived **happily** ever after. |
| slowly | The old man walked **slowly**. |

## 2. 程度副词（Adverbs of Degree）

回答"到什么程度？"

| 副词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| very | This book is **very** interesting. |
| quite | The movie was **quite** good. |
| rather | It's **rather** cold today. |
| almost | I have **almost** finished. |
| too | The coffee is **too** hot. |

## 3. 频率副词（Adverbs of Frequency）

回答"多久一次？"

| 频率程度 | 副词 | 例句 |
|----------|------|------|
| 100% | always | He **always** gets up early. |
| 80% | usually | I **usually** go to bed at 10. |
| 60% | often | She **often** visits her parents. |
| 40% | sometimes | We **sometimes** eat out. |
| 20% | seldom | He **seldom** watches TV. |
| 0% | never | She **never** tells a lie. |

## 4. 时间副词（Adverbs of Time）

回答"何时？"

| 副词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| now | I am busy **now**. |
| then | We lived in London **then**. |
| yesterday | He arrived **yesterday**. |
| today | What are you doing **today**? |
| soon | She will be back **soon**. |

## 5. 地点副词（Adverbs of Place）

回答"在哪里？"

| 副词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| here | Come **here**, please. |
| there | Put the book **there**. |
| upstairs | The children are playing **upstairs**. |
| everywhere | I looked **everywhere** for my keys. |
| outside | It's cold **outside**. |

## 6. 连接副词（Conjunctive Adverbs）

连接句子，表示逻辑关系。

| 副词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| however | 然而 | I was tired; **however**, I kept working. |
| therefore | 因此 | He was ill; **therefore**, he stayed home. |
| moreover | 而且 | The house is beautiful; **moreover**, it's affordable. |
| otherwise | 否则 | Hurry up; **otherwise**, you'll be late. |

## 7. 疑问副词（Interrogative Adverbs）

引导特殊疑问句。

| 副词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| when | 何时 | **When** did you arrive? |
| where | 何地 | **Where** do you live? |
| why | 为何 | **Why** are you late? |
| how | 如何 | **How** did you do it? |

# 二、副词在句中的位置

## 1. 频率副词的位置

| 位置 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| be 动词之后 | He is **always** on time. |
| 实义动词之前 | She **often** goes to the gym. |
| 助动词/情态动词之后 | I have **never** seen him. |

> [!NOTE] 规则
> **频率副词放在 be 动词之后、实义动词之前。**

例句：
- She is **always** happy.
- They **usually** have lunch at noon.
- I can **never** understand him.

## 2. 方式副词的位置

通常放在动词或宾语之后。

- He speaks English **fluently**.
- She closed the door **quietly**.
- They played the game **well**.

## 3. 程度副词的位置

通常放在所修饰词的前面。

- She is **very** beautiful.
- He runs **quite** fast.
- I **almost** forgot your name.

# 三、形容词变副词的规则

| 情况 | 规则 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般情况 | 形容词 + ly | quick → quickly, careful → carefully |
| 辅音字母 + y 结尾 | 变 y 为 i + ly | happy → happily, easy → easily |
| 以 le 结尾 | 变 le 为 ly | gentle → gently, simple → simply |
| 以 ic 结尾 | 加 ally | basic → basically, automatic → automatically |
| 不规则 | 特殊变化 | good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard |

例句：
- She answered the question **happily**.
- He explained it very **simply**.
- **Basically**, I agree with you.
- He runs very **fast**.
- She dances **well**.

> [!NOTE] 注意
> 有些词以 -ly 结尾但不是副词，而是形容词：friendly, lovely, lonely, daily, weekly。这些词不能加 -ly 变副词。

# 四、辨析

> [!NOTE] 辨析：hard vs hardly
> - **hard**（adv.）：努力地、猛烈地
> - **hardly**（adv.）：几乎不（= almost not）
>
> - He works **hard**.（他工作努力）
> - He **hardly** works.（他几乎不工作）
>
> 两者意思完全不同，不能混淆。

> [!NOTE] 辨析：late vs lately
> - **late**（adv.）：晚、迟
> - **lately**（adv.）：最近（= recently）
>
> - He arrived **late**.（他到晚了）
> - I haven't seen him **lately**.（我最近没见到他）

> [!NOTE] 辨析：high vs highly
> - **high**（adv.）：高（指空间/高度）
> - **highly**（adv.）：高度地（指程度/抽象）
>
> - The bird flew **high** in the sky.（鸟飞得高）
> - He is **highly** respected.（他备受尊敬）

> [!NOTE] 辨析：near vs nearly
> - **near**（adv.）：近（指空间/时间）
> - **nearly**（adv.）：几乎（= almost）
>
> - Come **near** and have a look.（走近看看）
> - I **nearly** forgot your birthday.（我差点忘了你的生日）
