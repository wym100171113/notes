# 非谓语动词 — 不定式（Infinitive）

不定式是**to + 动词原形**构成的非谓语形式，兼具名词、形容词、副词功能，表达**目的、将来、具体**的含义。

---

## 一、不定式的五种形式

| 形式 | 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般式 | **to do** | **to be done** |
| 进行式 | **to be doing** | — |
| 完成式 | **to have done** | **to have been done** |

- He seems **to know** the answer.（一般式：同时或之后）
- He seems **to be sleeping**.（进行式：正在发生）
- He seems **to have heard** the news.（完成式：先于谓语发生）
- The work needs **to be done** right away.（被动式）
- She is said **to have been promoted** recently.（完成被动式）

---

## 二、不定式的句法功能

### 1. 作主语

- **To learn** a foreign language is not easy.
- **To give up** now would be a mistake.

> [!NOTE] 形式主语 it 更常见  
> 英语中常用 `It is ... to do` 代替不定式作主语：  
> - It is not easy **to learn** a foreign language.

### 2. 作宾语

- I want **to travel** around the world.
- She decided **to quit** her job.

### 3. 作表语

- My goal is **to become** a doctor.
- The best way is **to practice** every day.

### 4. 作定语

- I have a lot of homework **to do**.
- He is the first person **to arrive**.

> 不定式作定语常表将来或目的：a book **to read**（要读的书）

### 5. 作状语（表目的 / 结果 / 原因）

- I came here **to help** you.（目的）
- He hurried to the station only **to find** the train had left.（结果，常带"意外"含义）
- She was happy **to hear** the news.（原因）

### 6. 作补语

- The teacher asked us **to be** quiet.（宾语补足语）
- He was asked **to leave**.（主语补足语）

---

## 三、不定式省略 to 的情况

| 情况 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| **感官动词**（see/hear/watch/feel/notice） + sb + do | I saw him **enter** the building. |
| **使役动词**（let/make/have） + sb + do | Let me **go**. / She made me **laugh**. |
| **had better / would rather** | You'd better **leave** now. |
| **why / why not** | Why **worry**? / Why not **try** again? |

> [!NOTE] 被动语态中 to 必须还原  
> 主动：I saw him **enter**. → 被动：He was seen **to enter**.

---

## 四、不定式的逻辑主语

当不定式的动作发出者与主句主语不同时，需要用 `for sb to do` 或 `of sb to do` 结构：

### for sb to do（不定式本身描述动作）

- It is important **for you to be** on time.
- It took three hours **for us to finish** the work.

### of sb to do（前面的形容词描述人的品质）

- It is kind **of you to help** me.（= You are kind to help me.）
- It was careless **of him to make** such a mistake.

> [!NOTE] 判断用 for 还是 of  
> 如果形容词能描述人的品质（kind, nice, brave, stupid, careless），用 **of**。  
> 如果形容词描述动作（important, necessary, difficult, easy），用 **for**。

## 五、核心辨析：不定式 vs 动名词（含义不同）

以下动词后接 to do 和 doing 含义完全不同：

| 动词 | + to do | + doing |
|------|---------|---------|
| **stop** | stop **to do**：停下来去做 | stop **doing**：停止做 |
| **remember** | remember **to do**：记得要去做 | remember **doing**：记得做过 |
| **forget** | forget **to do**：忘记要去做 | forget **doing**：忘记做过 |
| **try** | try **to do**：努力做 | try **doing**：尝试做 |
| **mean** | mean **to do**：打算做 | mean **doing**：意味着 |

- I stopped **to smoke**.（我停下来去抽烟）
- I stopped **smoking**.（我戒烟了）
- Remember **to lock** the door.（记得去锁门）
- I remember **locking** the door.（我记得锁过门了）
- I forgot **to call** him.（我忘了打电话）
- I'll never forget **meeting** her.（我永远不会忘记见过她）
- He tried **to open** the door.（他努力想把门打开）
- He tried **opening** the door with a key.（他尝试用钥匙开门）
- I meant **to call** you.（我本来打算打给你）
- Success means **working** hard.（成功意味着努力）

## 六、典型例句总汇

- It's never too late **to learn** a new skill.
- He pretended **to be reading** when the teacher came in.
- She is said **to have been** a famous actress.
- Let him **speak** — don't interrupt.
- They went to the airport **to pick up** their friend.
- I am sorry **to have kept** you waiting.
- The box is too heavy **to carry**.
- He was the last one **to leave** the office.
- It was brave **of him to save** the drowning child.
- Why not **take** a break? You've been working all day.
