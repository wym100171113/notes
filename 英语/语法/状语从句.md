# 状语从句（Adverbial Clause）

状语从句是在复合句中起**状语作用**的从句，用来修饰主句中的动词、形容词或副词，表示时间、地点、原因、目的、结果、条件、让步、方式和比较。

---

# 一、九种状语从句概述表

| 类型 | 常用连词 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| 时间 | when / while / as / before / after / since / until / till / as soon as / the moment / every time | I'll call you **when I arrive**. |
| 地点 | where / wherever | Sit **wherever you like**. |
| 原因 | because / since / as / for / now that | He was late **because he missed the bus**. |
| 目的 | so that / in order that | He got up early **so that he could catch the train**. |
| 结果 | so...that / such...that | He was **so tired that** he fell asleep. |
| 条件 | if / unless / as long as / on condition that / provided that | **If it rains**, I will stay home. |
| 让步 | although / though / even though / even if / while / no matter + wh- / wh-ever | **Although he is old**, he is very active. |
| 方式 | as / as if / as though | Do **as I told you**. |
| 比较 | than / as...as | He runs faster **than I do**. |

---

# 二、时间状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Time）

- **When** I arrived, he was reading.（当我到达时，他在看书。）
- Strike **while** the iron is hot.（趁热打铁。）
- **As** time goes by, we grow older.（随着时间的流逝，我们变老了。）
- I'll wait **until** you come back.（我会等到你回来。）
- **As soon as** he saw me, he waved.（他一看到我就挥手了。）
- We have been friends **since** we were children.（我们从小就是朋友。）

---

# 三、地点状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Place）

- Go back **where** you came from.（回到你来的地方去。）
- **Wherever** you go, I'll follow you.（无论你去哪里，我都跟着你。）
- Put the book **where** it was.（把书放回原处。）

---

# 四、原因状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Reason）

- He didn't come **because** he was sick.（他没来是因为他病了。）
- **Since** you are free today, let's go shopping.（既然你今天有空，我们去购物吧。）
- **As** it is raining, we'd better stay home.（由于下雨了，我们最好待在家里。）
- **Now that** everyone is here, let's begin.（既然大家都到了，我们开始吧。）

---

# 五、目的状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Purpose）

- He spoke loudly **so that** everyone could hear him.（他大声说话以便每个人都能听到。）
- They set off early **in order that** they could arrive on time.（他们早出发是为了能准时到达。）
- She saved money **so that** she could travel abroad.（她存钱是为了能出国旅游。）

---

# 六、结果状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Result）

- He was **so tired that** he fell asleep immediately.（他太累了以至于马上睡着了。）
- It was **such a beautiful day that** we decided to go out.（天气太好了以至于我们决定出去。）
- The box is **so heavy that** I can't carry it.（盒子太重了，我搬不动。）

---

# 七、条件状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Condition）

- **If** you work hard, you will succeed.（如果你努力，你会成功。）
- **Unless** you hurry up, you will be late.（除非你快点，不然你会迟到。）
- You can go out **as long as** you finish your homework.（只要你完成作业就可以出去。）
- **Provided that** no one objects, we'll start.（只要没人反对，我们就开始。）

---

# 八、让步状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Concession）

- **Although** he is young, he knows a lot.（虽然他很年轻，但他知道很多。）
- **Even though** it was cold, he went out without a coat.（即使很冷，他还是没穿外套就出去了。）
- **No matter what** happens, I'll support you.（无论发生什么，我都会支持你。）
- **However** hard he tried, he couldn't open the door.（无论他多努力，都打不开门。）

---

# 九、方式状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Manner）

- Do **as** I say.（照我说的做。）
- He talks **as if** he knew everything.（他说起话来好像他什么都懂。）
- She looked at me **as though** I were a stranger.（她看我的样子好像我是个陌生人。）

---

# 十、比较状语从句（Adverbial Clause of Comparison）

- He runs faster **than** I do.（他跑得比我快。）
- She is **as tall as** her mother.（她和她妈妈一样高。）
- The more you read, **the more** you know.（读得越多，知道得越多。）

---

# 十一、时态搭配规则（主将从现）

> [!NOTE] 核心规则
> 在**时间状语从句**和**条件状语从句**中，用**一般现在时**表示将来意义，主句用一般将来时。

| | 从句 | 主句 |
|--|------|------|
| 时态 | 一般现在时 | 一般将来时 |

例：
- **对**：If it **rains** tomorrow, I **will stay** home.
- **错**：~~If it will rain tomorrow, I will stay home.~~

- **对**：When he **arrives**, I **will tell** him the news.
- **错**：~~When he will arrive, I will tell him the news.~~

- **对**：As soon as she **gets** home, she **will call** you.
- **错**：~~As soon as she will get home, she will call you.~~

# 十二、辨析 1：because vs since vs as vs for

| 连词 | 语气强度 | 位置 | 用法 |
|------|:------:|------|------|
| because | 最强 | 句末/句中 | 直接原因，回答 why 提问 |
| since | 较弱 | 句首/句中 | 已知原因，"既然" |
| as | 较弱 | 句首/句中 | 附带原因，"由于" |
| for | 弱 | 句中 | 补充说明原因，不放句首 |

例：
- —Why were you late? —**Because** I missed the bus.（直接原因，回答 why）
- **Since** you are here, let's start.（既然你来了，我们开始吧。）
- **As** it was late, we went home.（天晚了，我们回家了。）
- He must be ill, **for** he looks so pale.（他肯定病了，因为他脸色苍白。）

> [!NOTE]
> for 是并列连词，连接的是并列句而非状语从句，不放在句首。

---

# 十三、辨析 2：although / though vs even though

| 对比维度 | although / though | even though |
|----------|:----------------:|:-----------:|
| 语气 | 一般让步 | 强调让步，语气更强 |
| 含义 | "虽然" | "即使" |

例：
- **Although** he is rich, he is not happy.（虽然他富有，但他不快乐。）
- **Even though** he is rich, he lives a simple life.（即使他富有，他仍过着简朴的生活。）
- She went on working **though** she was tired.（虽然累了，她还是继续工作。）

> [!NOTE] 不与 but 连用
> - **对**：Although he is poor, he is happy.
> - **对**：He is poor, **but** he is happy.
> - **错**：~~Although he is poor, but he is happy.~~

---

# 十四、辨析 3：so...that vs such...that

| 结构 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| so + 形容词/副词 + that | 修饰形容词或副词 | He is **so kind that** everyone likes him. |
| such + (a/an) + (形容词) + 名词 + that | 修饰名词短语 | He is **such a kind man that** everyone likes him. |

**关键公式：**

- **so** + adj./adv. + that
- **so** + many/much/few/little + 名词 + that
- **such** + (a/an) + (adj.) + 名词 + that

例：
- The box is **so heavy that** I can't lift it.（so + 形容词）
- He spoke **so fast that** I couldn't understand him.（so + 副词）
- It is **such a heavy box that** I can't lift it.（such + 名词短语）
- There were **so many people that** we couldn't get in.（so + many + 名词）

---

# 十五、典型例句总汇

- **When** spring comes, flowers bloom.（时间状语从句）
- **Wherever** you go, I will go with you.（地点状语从句）
- He succeeded **because** he worked hard.（原因状语从句）
- He ran fast **so that** he could catch the bus.（目的状语从句）
- She was **so excited that** she couldn't sleep.（结果状语从句）
- **If** it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic.（条件状语从句，主将从现）
- **Although** he failed many times, he never gave up.（让步状语从句）
- Do **as** the teacher tells you.（方式状语从句）
- Her English is better **than** mine.（比较状语从句）
