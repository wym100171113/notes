# 非谓语动词 — 动名词（Gerund）

动名词由 **动词 + -ing** 构成，本质是**动词的名词化**，在句中起名词作用。

---

## 一、动名词的四种形式

| 形式 | 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般式 | **doing** | **being done** |
| 完成式 | **having done** | **having been done** |

- **Swimming** is good exercise.（一般式）
- He denied **having stolen** the money.（完成式：先于谓语发生）
- She hates **being called** a liar.（被动式）
- He remembered **having been told** the news before.（完成被动式）

---

## 二、动名词的句法功能

### 1. 作主语

- **Reading** broadens your mind.
- **Smoking** is not allowed here.

> 常用形式主语 it：It is no use **crying** over spilt milk.

### 2. 作宾语

- I enjoy **listening** to music.
- She avoids **talking** about it.

### 3. 作表语

- His hobby is **collecting** stamps.
- The most important thing is **practicing** every day.

> [!NOTE] 动名词作表语 vs 进行时  
> - His job is **cleaning** the classroom.（动名词作表语，主语是"工作"）  
> - He is **cleaning** the classroom.（现在进行时，主语是"人"）

### 4. 作定语

动名词作定语表示**用途/功能**，而非动作：

- a **swimming** pool（= a pool for swimming）
- a **reading** room（= a room for reading）
- a **sleeping** bag（= a bag for sleeping）
- a **parking** lot（= a lot for parking）
- a **washing** machine（= a machine for washing）

---

## 三、动名词复合结构

当动名词需要自己的逻辑主语时，用**形容词性物主代词 / 名词所有格 + doing** 结构：

$$
\text{my/your/his/her/名词's + doing}
$$

- Do you mind **my smoking** here?（正式：my smoking）
- Do you mind **me smoking** here?（口语：me smoking）
- **Tom's being** late made the teacher angry.
- I appreciate **your coming** to see me.

> [!NOTE] 正式 vs 口语  
> 正式文体用形容词性物主代词（my/your/his），口语中常用宾格（me/you/him）。

---

## 四、只能接动名词的动词和短语

### 常见动词（只能接动名词，不能接不定式）

| 动词 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| enjoy | I enjoy **reading** novels. |
| finish | Have you finished **writing** the report? |
| mind | Would you mind **opening** the window? |
| suggest | I suggest **taking** a taxi. |
| avoid | He avoids **eating** junk food. |
| practice | She practices **playing** the piano every day. |
| consider | We are considering **buying** a new car. |
| imagine | Can you imagine **living** on Mars? |
| deny | He denied **stealing** the money. |
| admit | She admitted **making** a mistake. |
| risk | Don't risk **losing** everything. |
| keep | Keep **trying** and you'll succeed. |
| postpone | They postponed **holding** the meeting. |
| delay | Don't delay **making** a decision. |
| appreciate | I appreciate **your helping** me. |
| escape | He escaped **being punished**. |
| miss | I miss **living** by the sea. |

### 含 to 的介词短语（to 是介词，不是不定式符号）

| 短语 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| look forward to | I'm looking forward to **hearing** from you. |
| be used to | He is used to **getting up** early. |
| object to | I object to **being treated** like a child. |
| devote to | She devotes her time to **helping** others. |
| pay attention to | Pay attention to **listening**. |
| get down to | Let's get down to **working**. |
| be accustomed to | He is accustomed to **working** late. |
| be devoted to | She is devoted to **teaching**. |

> [!NOTE] 关键提示  
> 以上短语中的 `to` 是**介词**，后面必须接**名词或动名词**，不能接动词原形！  
> 错：I'm looking forward to ~~see~~ you.  
> 对：I'm looking forward to **seeing** you.

## 五、既可接动名词又可接不定式（含义不同）

以下动词接 doing 和 to do 含义完全不同（与不定式章节的辨析互补）：

| 动词 | + doing | + to do |
|------|---------|---------|
| **remember** | 记得做过 | 记得要去做 |
| **forget** | 忘记做过 | 忘记要去做 |
| **regret** | 后悔做过 | 遗憾地要做 |
| **try** | 尝试做（试验） | 努力做（付出努力） |
| **mean** | 意味着 | 打算做 |
| **stop** | 停止做 | 停下来去做 |

- I regret **telling** her the truth.（后悔告诉了她）
- I regret **to tell** you that you failed.（遗憾地告诉你）
- He stopped **smoking**.（他戒烟了）
- He stopped **to smoke**.（他停下来去抽烟）

> 详见：[[非谓语动词-不定式#五、核心辨析：不定式 vs 动名词（含义不同）]]

## 六、典型例句总汇

- **Learning** a new language takes patience and dedication.
- He admitted **having made** a serious mistake.
- She couldn't help **laughing** when she heard the joke.
- Do you mind **my opening** the window?
- We are all looking forward to **seeing** you at the party.
- I suggest **going** by train — it's faster and cheaper.
- He escaped **being caught** by hiding in the bushes.
- It is no use **arguing** with someone who won't listen.
- **Being criticized** in public made her very upset.
- Have you finished **packing** your luggage yet?
