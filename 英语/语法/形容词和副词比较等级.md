# 英语形容词和副词的比较等级

比较等级分为：**原级**（Positive）、**比较级**（Comparative）、**最高级**（Superlative）。

---

# 一、规则变化

## 1. 单音节词

| 规则 | 变化方式 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| 一般情况 | 词尾 + er / est | tall → taller → tallest |
| 以 e 结尾 | + r / st | large → larger → largest |
| 辅音字母 + y 结尾 | 变 y 为 i + er / est | happy → happier → happiest |
| 重读闭音节，末尾单辅音 | 双写辅音 + er / est | big → bigger → biggest |

例句：
- He is **taller** than his brother.
- She is the **happiest** girl in the class.
- This box is **bigger** than that one.
- The Nile is the **longest** river in the world.
- This problem is **simpler** than I thought.

## 2. 多音节词（三个或以上音节）

在词前加 **more** / **most**。

| 规则 | 变化方式 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| 三音节及以上 | more / most + 原级 | beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful |

例句：
- This dress is **more beautiful** than that one.
- She is the **most intelligent** student in the class.
- This is the **most expensive** restaurant in town.
- The problem is **more difficult** than I expected.
- He is the **most popular** teacher in the school.

## 3. 双音节词

多数双音节词用 **more / most**，但以 -y, -er, -ow, -le 结尾的可用 +er / +est。

- happy → happier → happiest
- clever → cleverer / more clever → cleverest / most clever
- narrow → narrower → narrowest

# 二、不规则变化

| 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 | 备注 |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| good / well | better | best | 好 |
| bad / badly / ill | worse | worst | 坏 |
| many / much | more | most | 多 |
| little | less | least | 少 |
| far | farther / further | farthest / furthest | 远 |
| old | older / elder | oldest / eldest | 老/旧 |

例句：
- He is **better** than me at math.
- This is the **worst** movie I've ever seen.
- I have **more** books than you.
- She earns **less** money than her husband.
- My house is **farther** from the station than yours.

---

# 三、原级比较句型

## 肯定：as + 原级 + as

- She is **as tall as** her mother.
- He runs **as fast as** a rabbit.
- This book is **as interesting as** that one.

## 否定：not as/so + 原级 + as

- He is **not as tall as** his brother.
- This test is **not so difficult as** I expected.
- She doesn't sing **as well as** you.

---

# 四、比较级句型

## 1. 比较级 + than

- He is **taller than** me.
- This city is **more crowded than** that one.

## 2. the + 比较级 + of the two

表示"两者中较……的一个"。

- He is **the taller of the two** brothers.
- This is **the cheaper of the two** options.

## 3. 比较级 + and + 比较级

表示"越来越……"。

- It's getting **colder and colder**.
- She is becoming **more and more beautiful**.
- The situation is getting **worse and worse**.

## 4. the + 比较级, the + 比较级

表示"越……，越……"。

- **The more** you practice, **the better** you will be.
- **The sooner**, **the better**.
- **The harder** you work, **the more** you will achieve.

---

# 五、最高级句型

## the + 最高级 + 范围（in / of / among）

- She is **the tallest** girl **in** the class.
- He is **the best** player **of** the team.
- This is **the most expensive** car **among** these three.

例句：
- Mount Everest is **the highest** mountain in the world.
- He is **the most careful** driver I know.
- This is **the worst** day of my life.
- She got **the highest** score in the exam.
- January is **the coldest** month of the year.

---

# 六、修饰比较级的词

以下词可用来修饰比较级，加强或减弱语气：

| 修饰词 | 语气 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| much | 强 | He is **much** taller than me. |
| even | 强 | It's **even** colder today. |
| far | 强 | This is **far** better than that. |
| a lot | 强 | She is **a lot** smarter. |
| a little | 弱 | It's **a little** warmer today. |
| a bit | 弱 | Could you speak **a bit** slower? |
| still | 强 | The next one is **still** better. |

例句：
- This book is **much** more interesting than that one.
- She is **a little** taller than her sister.
- The problem is **far** more complicated than we thought.
- It's getting **even** worse.
- He runs **a lot** faster than before.

# 七、辨析

> [!NOTE] 辨析：older vs elder
> - **older**：更老的、更旧的（可用于人或物，可作定语和表语）
> - **elder**：年长的（仅用于家人之间，只能作定语）
>
> - My **elder** brother is three years **older** than me.
> - This building is **older** than that one.（不能用 elder）

> [!NOTE] 辨析：farther vs further
> - **farther**：更远的（指**实际距离**）
> - **further**：更进一步的（指**抽象程度**）
>
> - My house is **farther** from the school than yours.（距离更远）
> - For **further** information, please call us.（更多信息 —— 抽象）
> - Let's discuss this **further** tomorrow.（进一步讨论 —— 抽象）

> [!NOTE] 易错点：比较级与最高级前的冠词
> - 比较级前一般不加 the，但特指"两者中较……的那个"时要加 the。
> - 最高级前一般必须加 the。
>
> - He is **taller** than me.（比较级，不加 the）
> - He is **the taller** of the two.（两者中较……的，加 the）
> - He is **the tallest** in the class.（最高级，加 the）
