# 倒装与强调（Inversion & Emphasis）

倒装（Inversion）是指把谓语的全部或部分放在主语之前。强调（Emphasis）则是通过特定句式来突出句子的某一部分。

---

# 一、完全倒装（Full Inversion）

完全倒装是指**整个谓语**放在主语之前。

## 1. here / there / now / then + 谓语 + 主语

- **Here comes the bus**.（公交车来了。）
- **There goes the bell**.（铃声响了。）
- **Now comes** your turn.（现在轮到你了。）
- **Then came** the big news.（接着来了重大消息。）

> [!NOTE] 主语为人称代词时不倒装
> - **Here he comes**.（他来了。）✓
> - ~~Here comes he.~~ ✗
> - **There she goes**.（她走了。）✓
> - ~~There goes she.~~ ✗

## 2. 表示方向/方位的副词 + 谓语 + 主语

up / down / in / out / away / off + 谓语 + 主语

- **Up went** the balloon.（气球升起来了。）
- **Out rushed** the children.（孩子们冲了出来。）
- **Away flew** the bird.（鸟飞走了。）

## 3. 地点状语 + 谓语 + 主语

- **On the wall hangs** a picture.（墙上挂着一幅画。）
- **At the foot of the hill lies** a lake.（山脚下有一个湖。）
- **In front of the house stands** a tall tree.（房子前面矗立着一棵大树。）

---

# 二、部分倒装（Partial Inversion）

部分倒装是指**助动词 / 情态动词 / be 动词**放在主语之前，其余谓语部分仍在主语之后。

## 1. 否定词在句首 → 部分倒装

否定词：never / seldom / rarely / little / hardly / scarcely / no sooner / not only / not until / nowhere / by no means / in no way / at no time

| 否定词 | 倒装结构 | 例句 |
|--------|----------|------|
| never | Never + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词 | **Never have I seen** such a beautiful sunset. |
| seldom | Seldom + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词 | **Seldom does he go** to the cinema. |
| hardly/scarcely...when | Hardly + had + 主语 + 过去分词...when | **Hardly had I arrived** when it began to rain. |
| no sooner...than | No sooner + had + 主语 + 过去分词...than | **No sooner had he left** than she called. |
| not only...but also | Not only + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词...but also | **Not only is he smart**, but he is also kind. |
| not until | Not until + 时间状语 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词 | **Not until then did I realize** my mistake. |

例：
- **Never** have I been to New York.（我从没去过纽约。）
- **Seldom** does she eat fast food.（她很少吃快餐。）
- **Hardly** had we started eating when the phone rang.（我们刚开始吃，电话就响了。）
- **No sooner** had he got home than it began to rain.（他一到家就开始下雨了。）
- **Not until** midnight did he come back.（直到半夜他才回来。）
- **By no means** should you lie.（你绝不应该撒谎。）

## 2. only + 状语在句首 → 部分倒装

- **Only then** did I realize the truth.（直到那时我才意识到真相。）
- **Only in this way** can we succeed.（只有这样我们才能成功。）
- **Only when** you try will you know it.（只有当你尝试了，你才会知道。）
- **Only after** the accident did he drive carefully.（只有出事后他才小心驾驶。）

> [!NOTE]
> only 修饰主语时不倒装：
> - Only he **knows** the answer.（只有他知道答案。）—— only 修饰主语 he，不倒装

## 3. so / neither / nor 表示「也 / 也不」→ 部分倒装

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| So + 助动词 + 主语 | ……也是 | He is a student, **so am I**. |
| Neither/Nor + 助动词 + 主语 | ……也不 | She can't swim, **neither/nor can I**. |

例：
- He likes music, and **so do I**.（他喜欢音乐，我也是。）
- She has been to Paris, and **so have I**.（她去过巴黎，我也去过。）
- He doesn't eat meat, and **neither do I**.（他不吃肉，我也不。）
- They won't give up, and **nor will we**.（他们不会放弃，我们也不会。）

## 4. 虚拟条件句省略 if → 倒装

省略 if 后，将 were / had / should 提前，构成部分倒装。

| 原句 | 倒装后 |
|------|--------|
| If I were you | **Were I** you |
| If I had known | **Had I** known |
| If he should come | **Should he** come |

例：
- **Were I you**, I would go.（如果我是你，我会去。）
- **Had I known** the truth, I would have told you.（如果我早知道真相，我会告诉你的。）
- **Should he come**, please tell me.（万一他来了，请告诉我。）

# 三、强调句（Emphatic Sentence）

## 1. It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分

该结构可以强调除谓语以外的**主语、宾语、状语**。

原句：I met him in the park yesterday.

| 强调部分 | 强调句 |
|----------|--------|
| 强调主语 | **It was I that/who** met him in the park yesterday. |
| 强调宾语 | **It was him that** I met in the park yesterday. |
| 强调地点状语 | **It was in the park that** I met him yesterday. |
| 强调时间状语 | **It was yesterday that** I met him in the park. |

更多例句：
- **It is** Tom **who/that** helped me.（是汤姆帮了我。—— 强调主语）
- **It was** the book **that** I wanted.（我要的是那本书。—— 强调宾语）
- **It is** because he is honest **that** I trust him.（正是因为他的诚实我才信任他。—— 强调原因状语）

## 2. do / does / did + 动词原形（强调谓语）

对谓语动词的强调只能通过 do/does/did + 动词原形来实现。

- I **do believe** you.（我确实相信你。）
- She **does love** music.（她确实热爱音乐。）
- He **did come** yesterday.（他昨天确实来了。）
- **Do be** careful!（一定要小心！）
- We **did enjoy** the party.（我们确实很享受聚会。）

# 四、辨析：强调句 It is...that vs 主语从句 It is...that

| 对比维度 | 强调句 | 主语从句 |
|----------|--------|---------|
| 去掉 It is...that 后 | 句子**依然完整** | 句子**不完整** |
| it 的作用 | 强调结构的一部分 | 形式主语 |
| that 后 | 完整的句子 | 不完整的从句 |

例：
- **强调句**：It was **yesterday** that I met him. → 去掉 It was...that → I met him yesterday. ✓ 完整
- **主语从句**：It is important **that we should study hard**. → 去掉 It is...that → we should study hard important. ✗ 不完整

> [!NOTE] 判断技巧
> 去掉 It is/was 和 that/who 后，调整语序：
> - 如果剩余部分可以组成一个完整句子 → 强调句
> - 如果剩余部分不能组成完整句子 → 主语从句

# 五、典型例句总汇

**完全倒装：**
- **Here comes** the bus!
- **On the top of the mountain stands** a temple.

**否定词倒装：**
- **Never** have I seen such courage.
- **Hardly** had I sat down when the phone rang.
- **Not until** he told me did I know the truth.
- **Not only** does she sing well, but she also dances beautifully.

**only 倒装：**
- **Only then** did I understand what he meant.

**强调句：**
- **It was** my mother **who** encouraged me most.
- **It was** at the airport **that** we met for the first time.
- I **do hope** you can come.

**虚拟倒装：**
- **Had** you come earlier, you would have met her.
