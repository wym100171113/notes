# 英语并列结构（Coordination / Compound Sentences）系统总结

并列结构指用并列连词将两个或多个**语法功能相同的成分**（词、短语、从句、分句）连接起来。

---

# 一、并列连词总览

| 连词 | 逻辑关系 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|------|
| **and** | 并列/顺承 | 和、然后 | He stood up **and** walked out. |
| **but** | 转折 | 但是 | I like coffee, **but** she prefers tea. |
| **or** | 选择/否则 | 或者、否则 | Hurry up, **or** you'll be late. |
| **nor** | 否定并列 | 也不 | He can't read, **nor** can he write. |
| **for** | 原因 | 因为 | He must be tired, **for** he has worked all day. |
| **so** | 结果 | 所以 | It was raining, **so** we stayed indoors. |
| **yet** | 转折（出乎意料） | 然而 | She is young, **yet** she is very capable. |

> [!NOTE] for 与 because 的区别
> - **for**：补充说明原因，不用于句首，语气较弱
>   - It must have rained, **for** the ground is wet.
> - **because**：直接因果关系，可放句首，语气强
>   - **Because** it rained, the ground is wet.

---

# 二、并列句的结构规则

## 并列句公式

$$
\text{简单句 + 并列连词 + 简单句}
$$

> **例句：**
> - I wanted to go, **but** I was too tired.
> - She studied hard, **so** she passed the exam.
> - You can stay here, **or** you can go home.

## 并列句中标点规则

> - 两个完整句子用并列连词连接时，连词前通常加**逗号**
>   - He is a teacher, **and** his wife is a doctor.
> - 若两个分句较短且关系紧密，可省略逗号
>   - I came and I saw.

---

# 三、并列结构的一致性原则

并列连词连接的各成分在**语法形式上必须一致**。

| 正确 ✅ | 错误 ❌ |
|---------|---------|
| She likes **swimming** and **dancing**.（doing + doing） | She likes **swimming** and **to dance**.（doing + to do） |
| He is **young** and **energetic**.（adj + adj） | He is **young** and **with energy**.（adj + 介词短语） |
| They went to the park **by bus** and **on foot**.（介词短语 + 介词短语） | They went **by bus** and **walked**.（介词短语 + 动词） |

> [!NOTE] 平行的本质
> 并列结构要求并列项"形似"——词性一致、结构对等。这是写作中最重要的语法原则之一。

---

# 四、并列句的省略

当并列分句中有重复的部分时，可以将相同部分省略。

> 完整：He is a doctor and **he is** a teacher.
> 省略：He is a doctor and a teacher.

> 完整：She can sing and **she can** dance.
> 省略：She can sing and dance.

> 完整：I have read the book and I have watched the movie.
> 省略：I have read the book and watched the movie.

> [!NOTE] 省略的原则
> 只要不引起歧义，相同的主语、助动词、情态动词均可省略。

# 五、关联并列连词（Correlative Conjunctions）

成对使用的并列连词，起强调作用。

| 关联连词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----------|------|------|
| **both...and...** | 两者都... | **Both** my father **and** my mother are teachers. |
| **either...or...** | 要么...要么... | You can choose **either** tea **or** coffee. |
| **neither...nor...** | 既不...也不... | **Neither** he **nor** I am wrong. |
| **not only...but also...** | 不仅...而且... | She is **not only** smart **but also** kind. |

> [!NOTE] 主谓一致规则
> - **both...and...** 谓语用复数
> - **either...or...** / **neither...nor...** / **not only...but also...** 谓语与**最近的主语**一致（就近原则）
>   - Neither he **nor** I **am** going.
>   - Not only the students **but also** the teacher **was** surprised.

# 六、并列连词辨析

## 1. and（并列） vs or（选择）

| 连词 | 逻辑 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **and** | 两者都成立 | I like tea **and** coffee.（两者都喜欢） |
| **or** | 两者选其一 | Would you like tea **or** coffee?（选一个） |

## 2. but（转折） vs yet（转折+惊讶）

| 连词 | 语气 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **but** | 一般转折，陈述事实 | He is old, **but** he is strong. |
| **yet** | 转折更强，带意外/惊讶 | He is 90 years old, **yet** he still runs every day. |

> **yet 也可与 and 或 but 连用：**
> - He is rich and **yet** unhappy.
> - She failed, but **yet** she didn't give up.

---

# 七、并列结构的常见错误

> [!NOTE] 错误 1：用逗号直接连接两个完整句子（逗号拼接）
> 错：I like reading, she likes writing.
> 对：I like reading, **and** she likes writing.
> 对：I like reading**;** she likes writing.（也可用分号）
> 对：I like reading. She likes writing.（也可分两句）

> [!NOTE] 错误 2：并列成分不平行
> 错：The job requires patience **and** to work hard.
> 对：The job requires **patience** and **hard work**.

> [!NOTE] 错误 3：not only... but also 语序
> not only 放句首时，该分句需**倒装**
> - Not only **does she** speak English, but she also speaks French.

# 八、典型例句总汇（高频）

- He stood up **and** walked out of the room.
- I like coffee, **but** she prefers tea.
- Hurry up, **or** you'll miss the bus.
- He can't sing, **nor** can he dance.
- It was late, **so** we went home.
- She is only 16, **yet** she runs her own business.
- **Both** Tom **and** Jerry are coming to the party.
- You must **either** apologize **or** leave.
- **Neither** the teacher **nor** the students know the answer.
- **Not only** did he finish the work, **but** he also helped others.
