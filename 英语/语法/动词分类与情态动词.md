# 动词分类与情态动词

英语动词按功能可分为四大类，其中情态动词是表达语气和态度的核心手段。

---

## 一、动词的四大分类

| 类别 | 英文名 | 核心特点 | 典型词 |
|------|--------|----------|--------|
| 实义动词 | Notional Verb | 有独立词汇意义，可单独作谓语 | run, eat, think, write |
| 系动词 | Linking Verb | 连接主语和表语，不能单独作谓语 | be, become, get, turn, look, sound, smell, taste, feel, seem, appear, remain, stay, keep |
| 助动词 | Auxiliary Verb | 帮助实义动词构成时态/语态/否定/疑问 | be, do, have |
| 情态动词 | Modal Verb | 表达说话人语气态度，后接动词原形 | can, must, should 等 |

> [!NOTE] 系动词完整列表  
> **状态类**：be  
> **变化类**：become, get, turn, go, grow  
> **感官类**：look, sound, smell, taste, feel  
> **表象类**：seem, appear  
> **持续类**：remain, stay, keep

---

## 二、情态动词完整表

| 情态动词 | 核心语义 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| **can** | 能力 / 许可 / 可能性 | She can swim very fast. |
| **could** | can 的过去式 / 委婉请求 / 过去能力 | Could you open the door? |
| **may** | 许可 / 可能性（较正式） | May I come in? |
| **might** | may 的过去式 / 极小可能性 | It might rain later. |
| **must** | 必须 / 肯定推测 | You must wear a seatbelt. |
| **shall** | 第一人称将来 / 征求意见 | Shall we dance? |
| **should** | 应该 / 按理应当 | You should see a doctor. |
| **will** | 将来 / 意愿 | I will help you. |
| **would** | will 的过去式 / 委婉 / 过去习惯 | He would often take a walk after dinner. |
| **need** | 需要（多用于否定/疑问） | You needn't worry. |
| **dare** | 敢（多用于否定/疑问） | How dare you say that? |
| **ought to** | 应该（比 should 语气更强） | You ought to apologize. |
| **have to** | 不得不（客观必须） | I have to get up early tomorrow. |

> [!NOTE] used to（过去习惯）  
> `used to` 严格来说不是情态动词，而是半助动词，但常与情态动词一起讨论。  
> 结构：`used to + 动词原形`，表示过去经常但现在不这样了。  
> 例：I used to smoke, but I quit last year.

## 三、情态动词核心用法详解

### 1. can / could

- **表能力**：I can speak three languages.（现在能力）
- **表许可**：You can leave now.（口语许可）
- **表可能性**：That can't be true.（否定推测，语气强烈）
- **委婉请求**：Could you do me a favor?（could 比 can 更礼貌）
- **过去能力**：When I was young, I could run 10 kilometers.（过去的一般能力）

### 2. may / might

- **表许可（正式）**：May I use your phone?
- **表可能性**：He may be at home now.
- **极小可能**：He might come, but I doubt it.
- **may have done** 表对过去的推测：She may have missed the bus.

### 3. must

- **表必须**：You must finish this report by Friday.
- **表肯定推测**：He must be tired after working 12 hours.
- **must have done** 表对过去的肯定推测：She must have forgotten about the meeting.（她肯定忘了）

### 4. should / ought to

- **表应该（建议）**：You should exercise more.
- **表按理应当（推测）**：The train should arrive at 10:00.
- **should have done** 表过去应该做而没做：I should have studied harder.

### 5. will / would / shall

- **will 表将来/意愿**：I will be 25 next year.
- **would 表委婉/过去习惯**：Would you like some tea?
- **shall 第一人称将来/征求意见**：Shall I open the window?

### 6. need / dare

- **need 作情态动词**（多用于否定/疑问）：You needn't come. / Need I go?
- **dare 作情态动词**（多用于否定/疑问）：I dare not tell him the truth. / How dare you!

## 四、情态动词表推测的强弱等级

情态动词用于推测时，语气从强到弱排列：

$$
\text{must（肯定） > will/would（很可能） > should/ought to（应该） > may/might/could（可能） > can't（不可能）}
$$

| 推测语气 | 情态动词 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| 肯定（99%） | must | He must be at home. The lights are on. |
| 很可能（80%） | will / would | That will be the postman. |
| 应该（60%） | should / ought to | The package should arrive today. |
| 可能（30%-50%） | may / might / could | It may rain this afternoon. |
| 不可能（0%） | can't | That can't be true! |

> [!NOTE] 对过去的推测  
> 结构：情态动词 + have + 过去分词  
> - must have done：过去一定做了  
> - may/might have done：过去可能做了  
> - could have done：过去本可以做（而没做）  
> - should have done：过去本应该做（而没做）  
> - can't have done：过去不可能做了

## 五、核心辨析

### 辨析 1：must（主观必须） vs have to（客观必须）

| | must | have to |
|------|------|---------|
| **语义** | 说话人主观认为必须 | 客观情况迫使 |
| **否定** | mustn't（禁止） | don't have to（不必） |
| **例句** | I must study harder.（我主观觉得必须努力） | I have to wear glasses.（客观需要，近视） |

- You **must** stop smoking. It's my order!（主观命令）
- I **have to** get up at 6:00 tomorrow because the meeting starts early.（客观需要）

### 辨析 2：can（现在能力） vs be able to（各时态能力）

- I **can** swim.（现在的能力）
- I **was able to** swim across the river yesterday.（过去具体某次做到了）
- I will **be able to** help you tomorrow.（将来，can 没有将来式）
- She has **been able to** solve the problem.（完成时态）

> [!NOTE] 关键区别  
> `can` 只有现在式和过去式（could），要表达其他时态必须用 `be able to`。

### 辨析 3：shall（第一人称将来/征求意见） vs will（各人称将来/意愿）

- **Shall** we go now?（征求意见，仅第一人称）
- I **shall** never forget your kindness.（第一人称将来，较正式/英式）
- He **will** arrive soon.（各人称均可）
- I **will** help you no matter what.（表意愿）

### 辨析 4：used to（过去习惯） vs would（过去反复动作）

- I **used to** live in Beijing.（过去住北京，现在不住了；used to 可描述状态）
- Every summer we **would** go to the beach.（过去每年夏天都去；would 仅描述反复动作，不表状态）
- 不能说：~~I would live in Beijing.~~（状态不能用 would）

## 六、典型例句总汇

- You **must** wear a helmet when riding a bike.
- **Could** you please pass me the salt?
- He **might** be stuck in traffic, so let's wait a few more minutes.
- You **should** take a break — you've been working all day.
- The ground is wet. It **must have rained** last night.
- I **used to** hate vegetables, but now I love them.
- You **needn't** bring anything to the party.
- She **can't** be serious about quitting her job.
- **Shall** I pick you up at the airport?
- If you **will** keep eating junk food, you'll gain weight.
