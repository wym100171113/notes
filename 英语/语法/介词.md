# 英语介词（Preposition）系统总结

介词用于表示名词/代词与句中其他词的关系，表示**时间、地点、方式、原因**等。

---

# 一、介词分类概述

## 1. 时间介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **at** | 点时间（几点、午夜、正午） | The meeting starts **at** 9 o'clock. |
| **on** | 具体某天（日期、星期、某天的上午/下午） | I was born **on** May 5th. / We'll meet **on** Monday. |
| **in** | 时间段（月份、年份、季节、世纪） | She will arrive **in** July. / He graduated **in** 2020. |
| **for** | 持续多久（时间段长度） | I have lived here **for** five years. |
| **since** | 自从（时间起点） | He has worked here **since** 2018. |
| **during** | 在...期间 | Don't talk **during** the exam. |
| **before** | 在...之前 | Please finish it **before** Friday. |
| **after** | 在...之后 | We went home **after** the party. |
| **until** | 直到（动作持续到某一时刻） | I'll wait **until** you come back. |
| **by** | 到...为止（截止时间） | You must finish it **by** tomorrow. |

> [!NOTE] for vs since
> - **for** + 时间段：I have studied English **for** three years.（学了三年）
> - **since** + 时间点：I have studied English **since** 2021.（从2021年开始学）

## 2. 地点介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **at** | 小地点/点位置 | I'll meet you **at** the bus stop. |
| **on** | 在...面上/线上 | The book is **on** the table. / The shop is **on** Main Street. |
| **in** | 大地点/空间内 | He lives **in** Beijing. / The pen is **in** the drawer. |
| **above** | 在...上方（不接触，不一定正上方） | The plane flew **above** the clouds. |
| **over** | 在...正上方（覆盖/跨越） | A bridge goes **over** the river. |
| **below** | 在...下方（不接触） | The temperature is **below** zero. |
| **under** | 在...正下方（覆盖） | The cat is **under** the table. |
| **between** | 在两者之间 | The park is **between** the school and the hospital. |
| **among** | 在三者及以上之间 | She is popular **among** her classmates. |
| **in front of** | 在...外部前面 | There is a tree **in front of** the house. |
| **in the front of** | 在...内部前部 | The driver sits **in the front of** the car. |

## 3. 方式介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **by** | 通过...方式（手段/交通） | I go to work **by** bus. / He succeeded **by** working hard. |
| **with** | 用（工具/身体部位） | Cut it **with** a knife. / I see **with** my eyes. |
| **in** | 用（语言/材料/声音） | She wrote the letter **in** English. / Paint it **in** red. |
| **through** | 通过（途径/过程） | We learned the news **through** the Internet. |

## 4. 原因介词

| 介词短语 | 用法 | 例句 |
|----------|------|------|
| **because of** | 因为（最常用，中性） | The game was canceled **because of** the rain. |
| **due to** | 由于（正式，常作表语） | His success is **due to** hard work. |
| **owing to** | 由于（正式，常作状语） | **Owing to** bad weather, the flight was delayed. |
| **thanks to** | 多亏/由于（褒义为主） | **Thanks to** your help, I passed the exam. |

# 二、常见介词搭配

## 1. 动词 + 介词

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| look at | 看 | Please **look at** the blackboard. |
| listen to | 听 | She likes to **listen to** music. |
| depend on | 依赖 | It **depends on** the weather. |
| belong to | 属于 | This book **belongs to** me. |
| agree with | 同意（某人） | I **agree with** you completely. |
| apply for | 申请 | He wants to **apply for** that job. |
| wait for | 等待 | I'll **wait for** you at the gate. |
| care about | 关心 | She doesn't **care about** money. |
| think of | 想到/想起 | What do you **think of** this idea? |

> [!NOTE] agree with vs agree to
> - agree **with** + 人/意见：I agree **with** him.
> - agree **to** + 计划/建议：He agreed **to** the plan.

## 2. 形容词 + 介词

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be good at | 擅长 | He is **good at** math. |
| be interested in | 对...感兴趣 | She is **interested in** history. |
| be afraid of | 害怕 | The child is **afraid of** dogs. |
| be proud of | 为...自豪 | We are **proud of** our team. |
| be famous for | 因...著名 | Paris is **famous for** its cuisine. |
| be different from | 与...不同 | My opinion is **different from** yours. |
| be angry with | 生（某人）的气 | She was **angry with** her son. |
| be satisfied with | 对...满意 | I am **satisfied with** the result. |

# 三、介词辨析

## 1. in / on / at 的空间含义

| 介词 | 空间含义 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **at** | 在...点（视为一个点） | He is standing **at the door**.（在门口） |
| **on** | 在...面上（接触表面） | There is a sign **on the door**.（在门上） |
| **in** | 在...里面（空间内部） | The coat is **in the room**.（在房间里） |

> [!NOTE] 用空间概念理解时间
> - **at** 点时间 → at 3:00（钟点视为一个点）
> - **on** 具体天 → on Monday（天视为一个面）
> - **in** 时间段 → in July（月份视为一个空间）

## 2. between vs among

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **between** | 两者之间 | The secret is **between** you and me. |
| **among** | 三者及以上之间 | He is the tallest **among** the students. |

## 3. except / besides / except for

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **except** | 除了...（不包括，从整体中排除） | Everyone went **except** Tom.（Tom没去） |
| **besides** | 除了...（还包括，累加） | **Besides** English, he speaks French.（两种都会） |
| **except for** | 除了...（整体中的细节瑕疵） | The essay is good **except for** a few spelling mistakes. |

## 4. across vs through

| 介词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **across** | 从表面穿过（横穿平面） | We walked **across** the street. |
| **through** | 从内部穿过（穿越空间） | The river flows **through** the forest. |

## 5. for vs since

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **for** | + 时间段（多久） | I have been here **for** two hours. |
| **since** | + 时间点（从何时起） | I have been here **since** 8 o'clock. |

# 四、易错点总结

> [!NOTE] 易错点 1：in / on / at 时间用法
> 错：I will go on 3:00.
> 对：I will go **at** 3:00.
> 规则：具体几点用 **at**，具体某天用 **on**，月份年份用 **in**

> [!NOTE] 易错点 2：arrive 后的介词
> - arrive **at** + 小地点：arrive at the station
> - arrive **in** + 大地点：arrive in London
> - 没有 arrive **to** 的用法

> [!NOTE] 易错点 3：married 后的介词
> 错：She married with a doctor.
> 对：She is married **to** a doctor. / She married a doctor.

> [!NOTE] 易错点 4：discuss / enter / reach 不加介词
> 错：Let's discuss **about** the plan.
> 对：Let's discuss the plan.（discuss 直接加宾语）
> 同理：enter the room（不加 into），reach the top（不加 to）

# 五、典型例句总汇（高频）

- The meeting is **at** 3:00 **on** Monday **in** June.
- I have been learning English **for** five years.
- He has lived here **since** he was born.
- Please finish the report **by** Friday.
- Don't walk **across** the busy street.
- The train goes **through** the tunnel.
- Everyone passed the exam **except** Tom.
- **Besides** being a writer, she is also a painter.
- He succeeded **by** working day and night.
- She wrote the letter **in** French.
