# 定语从句（Attributive Clause）

定语从句是用一个句子修饰**名词或代词**的从句，被修饰的名词/代词称为**先行词**，引导从句的词称为**关系词**。

---

# 一、关系代词

关系代词在从句中**代替先行词**，并作主语、宾语、定语等成分。

| 关系代词 | 先行词 | 在从句中充当 | 示例 |
|----------|--------|:----------:|------|
| who | 人 | 主语 | The man **who** is standing there is my teacher. |
| whom | 人 | 宾语 | The man **whom** you met is my teacher. |
| whose | 人 / 物 | 定语（所有格） | The boy **whose** father is a doctor is my friend. |
| which | 物 / 动物 | 主语 / 宾语 | The book **which** is on the desk is mine. |
| that | 人 / 物 | 主语 / 宾语 | He is the man **that** helped me. |
| as | 人 / 物 / 整句 | 主语 / 宾语 | **As** is known to all, she is honest. |

---

# 二、关系副词

关系副词在从句中作**状语**。

| 关系副词 | 先行词 | 相当于 | 示例 |
|----------|--------|--------|------|
| when | 时间名词 | at/in/on which | I'll never forget the day **when** we met. |
| where | 地点名词 | at/in/on which | This is the house **where** he was born. |
| why | reason | for which | That is the reason **why** he was late. |

---

# 三、限制性定语从句 vs 非限制性定语从句

## 1. 限制性定语从句（无逗号）

去掉后句意不完整，是句子不可缺少的部分。

- He is the man **who helped me**.（他就是帮我的那个人。）
- The book **that I bought yesterday** is interesting.（我昨天买的那本书很有趣。）
- This is the place **where I grew up**.（这是我长大的地方。）

## 2. 非限制性定语从句（有逗号）

去掉后句意仍然完整，只起补充说明作用，**不能用 that**。

- My father, **who is a doctor**, works hard.（我爸爸是医生，他工作很努力。）
- Beijing, **which is the capital of China**, is a beautiful city.（北京是中国的首都，它很美。）
- She gave me a gift, **which made me very happy**.（她给了我一份礼物，这让我很开心。）

> [!NOTE] 非限制性定语从句不能用 that
> - My mother, **who** is a teacher, loves reading. ✓
> - My mother, ~~that~~ is a teacher, loves reading. ✗

---

# 四、介词 + 关系代词

当关系代词在从句中作**介词宾语**时，可将介词提前。

- The house **in which** he lives is old.（他住的那所房子很旧。）
- The man **with whom** I talked is my teacher.（和我谈话的那个人是我的老师。）
- This is the reason **for which** he was late.（这就是他迟到的原因。）
- The desk **at which** he sits is broken.（他坐的那张桌子坏了。）
- The company **for which** she works is famous.（她工作的那家公司很有名。）

---

# 五、只能用 that 不能用 which 的情况

| 情况 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 先行词被**最高级**修饰 | This is the best book **that** I have ever read. |
| 先行词被**序数词**修饰 | He is the first person **that** arrived. |
| 先行词被 the only / the very / the last 修饰 | He is the only person **that** can help us. |
| 先行词是**不定代词**（all/much/little/everything/nothing 等） | All **that** glitters is not gold. |
| 先行词**既有又有人又有物** | They talked about the people and things **that** they remembered. |
| 以 who / which 开头的疑问句 | Who is the man **that** is standing there? |

---

# 六、只能用 which 不能用 that 的情况

| 情况 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 非限制性定语从句 | My house, **which** is very old, needs repairing. |
| 介词后 | The room in **which** they are studying is big. |

---

# 七、辨析 1：that vs which

| 对比维度 | that | which |
|----------|:----:|:-----:|
| 先行词范围 | 人 / 物 | 仅物 |
| 非限制性定语从句 | ❌ | ✅ |
| 介词后 | ❌ | ✅ |
| 先行词被最高级/序数词/the only 修饰 | ✅ 必须用 | ❌ |
| 先行词是不定代词 | ✅ 必须用 | ❌ |
| 先行词既有人又有物 | ✅ 必须用 | ❌ |

例：
- This is the best film **that** I have seen.（最高级，必须用 that）
- The book, **which** I bought yesterday, is great.（非限制性，必须用 which）
- The house in **which** he lives is large.（介词后，必须用 which）

---

# 八、辨析 2：who vs whom

| 对比维度 | who | whom |
|----------|:---:|:----:|
| 格 | 主格 | 宾格 |
| 在从句中作 | 主语 | 宾语 |
| 口语 | 常用 | 常被 who 替代 |

例：
- The man **who** is talking to her is my boss.（who 作 talking 的主语）
- The man **whom** I met yesterday is a doctor.（whom 作 met 的宾语）
- The girl **who(m)** you are looking for is in the library.（口语中可用 who 代替 whom）

> [!NOTE]
> 介词后必须用 whom：
> - The man **with whom** I talked is my teacher. ✓
> - The man ~~with who~~ I talked is my teacher. ✗

---

# 九、辨析 3：as vs which（指代整个主句）

| 对比维度 | as | which |
|----------|:--:|:----:|
| 词义 | "正如" | 无"正如"之意 |
| 位置 | 句首 / 句中 / 句末 | 句中 / 句末 |
| 情感色彩 | 客观陈述 | 可带惊讶等情感 |

例：
- **As** is known to all, China is a great country.（as 指代整个主句，放句首）
- He failed the exam, **which** surprised us all.（which 指代整个主句，不放在句首）
- **As** we expected, he won the match.（正如我们预料的那样，他赢了比赛。）
- She was late again, **which** made her boss angry.（她又迟到了，这让她老板很生气。）
- **As** the saying goes, practice makes perfect.（正如谚语所说，熟能生巧。）

---

# 十、典型例句总汇

- The girl **who is singing** is my sister.（who 作主语）
- The book **which I read** was very moving.（which 作宾语）
- This is the school **where I studied** for three years.（关系副词，地点）
- I'll never forget the day **when I first met you**.（关系副词，时间）
- Everything **that** can be done has been done.（不定代词先行词，必须用 that）
- The boy **whose bike was stolen** is crying.（whose 表示所有格）
- Tom, **who is my best friend**, moved to another city.（非限制性定语从句）
- He didn't pass the exam, **which disappointed his parents**.（which 指代整句）
